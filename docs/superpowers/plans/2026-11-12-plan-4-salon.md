# Plan 4 — Salon (services, appointments, packages, credits, staff/commissions)

Source spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-business-agnostic-design.md`
(§3 Appointment/Package/staff fields, §4 `/appointments` `/sell` `/customers`
rows, §6).

**Goal**: A salon store sells services (no stock moves, optional staff
attribution with commission), books appointments on a day view, converts a
booked appointment (or a walk-in) into a visit sale in one tap, sells service
packages whose credits get consumed line-by-line ("use credit"), and reports
per-staff commissions. Retail/pharmacy/restaurant stores see none of it.

**Deliverable you can test**: switch a shop to salon → `/sell` treats products
marked as services correctly (no stock decrement, duration shown, staff picker
per line when `commissions` is on); `/appointments` day view shows the
schedule with walk-in + convert-to-sale; packages editor + package purchase;
"Use credit" on the pay flow zeroes the line and decrements the customer's
remaining credits; refunding a credit line restores it; customer detail shows
visit history (orders) + visit notes + package credit balance; staff editor
gains skills/schedule/commissionPct; reports gain a commissions section;
receipts show staff names on service lines and a credit line; a synced second
device receives `appointments` and `packages` (staff was already synced).

**Out of scope**: per-appointment resource/room booking (one service per
time-slot line only); automatic SMS/WhatsApp reminders; commission *payout*
records (the report is informational); staff photo upload changes; pharmacy/
restaurant features; staff schedule *enforcement* at booking time (schedule
rows are stored and displayed, not validated — flagged as future work per
spec "no schedule enforcement v1").

## Modeling decisions (fixed before tasks)

- **Services**: `Product.kind?: "product" | "service"` (default product).
  Services skip ALL stock logic in `placeOrder` and `refundOrder` (no moves,
  no stock decrement/restore, no batch participation). Product edit shows a
  Service toggle + `durationMin` when the salon preset or `services` feature
  is on. The sell grid shows duration under the price for services.
- **Staff attribution & commissions**: each cart line may carry a staff id.
  Cart is extended to `CartLine.staffId?` (Plan 2's cart identity key is
  unchanged; staffId rides along). On `placeOrder`, `OrderItem.serviceStaffId`
  + `commissionAmt = line.price × staff.commissionPct / 100` (0 when no
  staff or no `commissions` feature). `Order.staffId` = primary staff (the
  most-repeated line staff, else undefined) — stamped for reporting; the
  server column lands in `0016`.
- **No new Feature flag for schedules**: the code Feature union is
  kebab-case and has no `staffSchedules` member. Schedule/skills editing in
  the staff screen is gated behind `hasFeature("services")` (skills) and
  `hasFeature("commissions")` (commissionPct); schedule rows display on
  `/appointments` regardless (salon preset always has `services`).
  Documented as a modeling decision to avoid Feature-union churn.
- **Appointments**: `Appointment { id, customerId, staffId?, serviceLines:
  [{ productId, price }], start: ISO datetime, durationMin, status:
  "booked" | "arrived" | "in-service" | "done" | "no-show" | "cancelled",
  notes? }`. Store: `upsertAppointment(a)`, `setAppointmentStatus(id, s)`,
  `deleteAppointment(id)`. Walk-in = appointment created directly in
  `in-service` status (one tap on `/appointments` with no customer → optional
  anonymous). **Convert to sale** = load the appointment's service lines into
  the cart (staff attached per line), tag the cart with the appointment id;
  the normal pay flow creates the visit Order via `placeOrder` and then
  marks the appointment `done` (store helper `convertAppointmentToOrder`).
  Cancelling/abandoning the cart just detaches the tag; the appointment stays
  in its status.
- **Packages**: `Package { id, name, price, credits: [{ productId, qty }] }`.
  Selling a package = an order line whose product is the package pseudo-item
  (`OrderItem.packageId`, line price = package price); completing such an
  order appends `{ packageId, remaining: Σ credits qty }` to
  `customer.packageCredits`. **Using a credit**: sell-screen "use credit"
  flag on a line (`OrderItem.useCredit`) when the customer has a matching
  credit entry — the line's `price` becomes 0 and the package's `remaining`
  decrements by the line qty at `placeOrder` (never below 0; over-consumption
  prices the excess at normal price). Credit lines are commission-eligible
  at 0 amount.
- **Refunds vs credits**: refunding a credit-consumed line RESTORES the
  credit (remaining += line qty, same packageId); refunding the package
  purchase line restores nothing (un-consumed credits of that package are
  voided by setting their remaining to 0; consumed stays consumed). Refund
  of a package purchase line only appears for orders that bought one.
- **Visits & visit notes**: an order on a customer IS a visit (no new
  entity). Customer detail lists their orders (today first) and allows
  appending a note to `visitNotes: { t, text }[]` (newest first). Anonymous
  walk-ins create orders without `customerId` as today.
- **Customer credits storage**: `customer.packageCredits` lives on the
  synced customer row (jsonb `package_credits`); no separate credit table —
  single-writer-per-customer is the same tradeoff as cart, acceptable per
  spec §3.
- **Commissions report**: per staff member for a date range: visits served
  (orders with ≥1 line attributed), service revenue (Σ attributed line
  prices incl. 0-price credits listed separately), commission = Σ
  `commissionAmt`. Informational only; no payout records.

## Task 1 — Data core (types, migration 0016, store actions, sync)

Files: `lib/types.ts`, `lib/store.ts`, `lib/staff.ts`,
`backend/supabase/migrations/0016_salon.sql`, `lib/sync/outbox.ts`

1. Types: Product += `kind?: "product" | "service"`, `durationMin?: number`;
   Customer += `visitNotes?: { t, text }[]`, `packageCredits?:
   { packageId, remaining }[]`; Order += `staffId?`, `appointmentId?`;
   OrderItem += `serviceStaffId?`, `commissionAmt?: number`,
   `useCredit?: boolean`, `packageId?: string`; Staff (lib/staff.ts) +=
   `skills?: string[]`, `schedule?: { day: number; start: string; end:
   string }[]`, `commissionPct?: number`; new `Appointment` + `Package`
   entity types; store gains `appointments: Appointment[]`,
   `packages: Package[]` (synced).
2. Store actions: `upsertAppointment` / `setAppointmentStatus` /
   `deleteAppointment`; `upsertPackage` / `deletePackage`;
   `upsertStaff` extended for the new fields; credit helpers
   `creditBalanceOf(customerId, packageId)`. `placeOrder` branches: skip
   stock entirely for service lines; compute `commissionAmt` per line;
   package-purchase line → append credits; `useCredit` lines → price 0 +
   decrement remaining; stamp `Order.staffId`/`appointmentId`. `refundOrder`
   branches: service lines skip restock; credit lines restore; package
   purchase line voids credits per decision. `partialize` includes
   `appointments` + `packages`; cart gains transient `cartStaffId?` per
   line + `cartAppointmentId?` (cleared on `clearCart`, NOT persisted).
3. Staff sync payload: pass the three new fields through `staffLocal`/
   merge mappers in `engine.ts` (staff already registered).
4. Migration `0016_salon.sql`: `appointments` (id uuid, shop_id, customer_id,
   staff_id, service_lines jsonb, start timestamptz, duration_min int,
   status text check in set, notes text, timestamps) + RLS + realtime;
   `packages` (id uuid, shop_id, name, price numeric, credits jsonb,
   timestamps) + RLS + realtime; products += `kind text`, `duration_min int`;
   customers += `visit_notes jsonb`, `package_credits jsonb`; order_items +=
   `service_staff_id uuid`, `commission_amt numeric`, `use_credit boolean`,
   `package_id uuid`; orders += `staff_id uuid`, `appointment_id uuid`;
   staff += `skills jsonb`, `schedule jsonb`, `commission_pct numeric`.
   All nullable / with defaults.
5. Sync registration for `appointments` + `packages` in `engine.ts`
   (TABLES, emptyMap, push, pull mappers, stamps, fingerprint, asks,
   watchers) and `outbox.ts` `DEPS`.

Verify: tsc, eslint. Manual: retail shop unchanged (no appointments/packages
rows); salon shop's existing data loads with new nullable fields absent.
